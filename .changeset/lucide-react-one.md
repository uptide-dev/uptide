Add a verified lucide-react 0.x to 1.x migration pack. Brand icons removed in 1.x are found at their imports and every use, with a guide whose replacement choice goes to the owner; icon names dropped during 0.x are renamed at the import or member; deep `dist/` imports, untyped icon lookups, the `absoluteStrokeWidth` and `IconNode` deprecations, and the `aria-hidden` default for repositories before 0.489.0 are reported. Lucide's lockstep packages move together and the consumer's build is required. Verified against three public repositories, one of them a negative control.
