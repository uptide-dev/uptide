`uptide pack test` applies a stricter verified gate: at least five expected breaking sites across the public ground-truth repositories, besides two repositories and no breaking false positive. Upgrades the compiler cannot see are gated on their behavior notes instead. Mechanical rules no real site exercises are recorded as unexercised, and `check` and `fix` run their sites as assisted. `@sentry/browser` and `@sentry/react` become candidates (fewer than five real breaking sites). A pack can lower the minimum only with a written reason in its RESEARCH.md. The README packs table shows each pack's breaking-site count. The output shows the gate's class, site counts and every reason a pack is a candidate.
